# Myelin Launch Video Design

## Style Prompt

Greek-tech procedural learning system. Dark navy canvas, gold myelin sheath/sigil motif, teal signal path, restrained terminal overlays, crisp serif wordmark energy, and precise engineering motion. The video should feel like a serious open-source systems project, not a generic AI dashboard.

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#08111F` |
| Panel navy | `#101D2F` |
| Myelin gold | `#E9A936` |
| Bright gold | `#F8D36A` |
| Signal teal | `#2DD4BF` |
| Text | `#F8FAFC` |
| Muted text | `#94A3B8` |

## Typography

- Display: Georgia or a sharp editorial serif.
- Mono/UI: SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use generic brain icons or neural-network webs.
- Do not imply Myelin is an orchestrator or generic memory database.
- Do not claim HTTP/port transport in the video.
- Do not use loud gradients, meme pacing, or unverifiable benchmark claims.
